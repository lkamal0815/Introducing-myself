Introducing Liz Kamal!

Liz recently switched to study journalism after nearly a decade running the media relations program at a PR firm. She wants to focus on international reporting and dive into how countries interconnect and why that matters to a U.S. audience.

Here are some of my favorite stories:

  1. Warwick’s AppleFest Draws in Hundreds, Despite This Year’s Lack of Apples - This is a hyperlocal story, but has the shock value that is really intriguing. Growing up on the East Coast, we always picked apples but never realized what goes on behind the scenes.
  2. How New Yorkers Rallied to Answer One Teen’s Cry for Career Help - This is just such a fun, feel good story, even if it was for a college essay. I spoke with the WSJ team who worked on this, and it was a full team effort to make some good news.
  3. The surprising way walking can benefit your brain - Another fun story backed in science! I also sent to my friends to convince them to go on more walks, and it worked!
